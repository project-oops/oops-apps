# The start-up controls card (common/controls_card.h): SuperTux's game-controller
# defaults (upstream/src/control/game_controller_manager.cpp).
Left stick / D-pad = Walk, duck, climb, enter doors
Cross = Jump
Square = Run, grab, fire
Right stick = Look around
L1 / R1 = Look left / right
Options = Pause
> Menus: Cross selects, Circle goes back. On the map, Cross enters a level.
