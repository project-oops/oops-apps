Left stick / D-pad = Move
Right stick = Rotate the camera
Cross = Jump
Square = Kick
Circle = Pick up / drop
Triangle = Launch buddy
R3 = Camera mode
Options = Pause
> Menus: D-pad or stick, Cross selects, Circle goes back.
