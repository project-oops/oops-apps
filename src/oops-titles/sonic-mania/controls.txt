# The start-up controls card (common/controls_card.h): RSDKv5's SDL2 pad mapping and
# the game's own reads (Objects/Global/Player.c).
Left stick / D-pad = Move
Cross / Circle / Square = Jump
Down + jump = Spin dash
Triangle = Swap character (Encore)
Options = Pause
> Menus: Cross confirms, Circle goes back. Options, Controls shows the pad.
